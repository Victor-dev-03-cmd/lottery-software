Place the following image assets in this directory before building:

  icon.png           - 1024x1024 app icon (PNG, no transparency)
  splash.png         - 1284x2778 splash screen (PNG)
  adaptive-icon.png  - 1024x1024 adaptive icon foreground (PNG with transparency)
  favicon.png        - 48x48 favicon (PNG)

Design note: use #CF291D (dark red) as the background/accent colour to match the desktop app.

You can generate placeholders quickly with:
  npx expo-image-utils generate-app-icons ./assets/icon.png
or supply your own branded assets.
